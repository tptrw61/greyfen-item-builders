This companion report covers the 66 “Reasonably sure” and 14 “Can't fully match” items — every item whose build carries at least one gap. The 52 exact matches and the 12 incompatible entries are not repeated here; see the main report for those.

Part 1 is a taxonomy: each distinct reason, what it means, why the builders cannot express it, and what would close the gap. Part 2 is per-item: the catalogue entry beside what the builder actually produced, the exact effects used to build it, and the specific catalogue sentence behind each gap.

Reading the markers: Reasonably sure means every number in the stat line was reproduced and the gap is a rule with no builder equivalent — these were saved as JSON + HTML. Can't fully match means a defining property could not be represented at all — saved as JSON only.

■ Barding — Horse: animal barding has no builder location (built as chest with matching defenses)

5 items
The six builders cover humanoid equipment: Head, Chest, Hands and Feet armor, plus shields and weapons. Barding is mount armor with its own slot concept (and, for a chamfron, its own sub-location on the animal). There is no barding builder, so this was built with the chest builder purely to carry the correct defense numbers — the resulting item would occupy a creature's chest slot, which is wrong for barding.
What would close the gap: A barding builder with mount locations.
Affects: Chamfron only, Leather barding, Mail / scale / lamellar barding, Padded caparison, Plate barding

■ damage 7d10 (max 70) is not a multiple of 4; built closest 18d4 (max 72)

1 item
Builder damage is assembled from 1d4 units: each stack of Slam (Physical) or Harsh (other types) adds 1d4, so a build's maximum damage is always a multiple of 4, and the displayed dice must divide that maximum evenly. These two entries have maxima that are not multiples of 4, so the exact dice cannot be produced — the closest achievable total was built instead. This is the only place in the whole catalogue where the mismatch is purely numeric.
What would close the gap: Damage units that are not restricted to multiples of 4.
Affects: Poleaxe

▲ situational favor/disfavor not modelled

17 items
Favor / disfavor is Utopia's circumstantial die mechanic, granted only when a described situation holds (from horseback, against an unaware target, when braced against a rest, when an allied pike user is adjacent). Every builder effect is unconditional once the item is equipped — none takes a trigger condition — so there is no way to attach “1 favor when X” to a build.
What would close the gap: A “Favored (circumstance)” effect carrying a free-text or enumerated trigger.
Affects: Arbalest, Boar spear / winged spear, Composite horse bow, Flail, military one-handed, Flail, military two-handed, Great pike, 16-20 ft, Hanger / cutlass, Improvised maul, Improvised small blade, Meteor hammer, Military fork, Partisan, Ranseur / spetum, Sabre / tulwar, Sword-cane, War bow, exceptional draw, Woodsman's felling axe

▲ material-specific double damage approximated with Ship Wrecker (which applies to all non-creature objects)

8 items
The catalogue limits the doubling to a material class (unattended wood, rope, hide, rigging, brush, canvas). The builder's Ship Wrecker doubles damage against all targets other than creatures. Ship Wrecker was applied, so the build is broader than the catalogue entry, not narrower — this is an overshoot rather than a missing rule.
What would close the gap: A target-class parameter on Ship Wrecker (e.g. Ship Wrecker (wood)).
Affects: Battleaxe, Falchion, Halberd, Hand axe / hatchet, Hanger / cutlass, Heavy two-handed weapon (greatsword, greataxe, or great hammer), Long axe / voulge, Woodsman's felling axe

▲ armor-bypass rider not modelled

7 items
These weapons ignore or halve a specific amount of Armour Defense, often gated behind an extra action or a target condition (Grappled, Prone, unaware). The builder's nearest tool is Penetrative, which ignores a chosen damage type's defense entirely (+60 RP, minimum rarity Extraordinary). It is all-or-nothing: it cannot ignore “3 points”, cannot halve, and cannot be gated behind spending an extra action or a target's condition. Applying Penetrative would have made these weapons far stronger than the catalogue intends.
What would close the gap: A partial armor-bypass effect taking an amount (or a halving flag) plus an optional activation cost/condition.
Affects: Armour-piercing dagger (awl, misericorde, or rondel), Awl pike, Estoc, Halberd, Lucerne hammer, Poleaxe, Warhammer / horseman's pick

▲ disarm/prone alternate-attack not modelled

5 items
The note offers an alternate attack mode: instead of dealing damage, make an opposed contest to disarm the target, pull it, or knock it Prone. A builder weapon describes one thing — an attack that deals its damage. There is no effect that swaps damage for a contest, and none that applies the Prone or Disarmed condition.
What would close the gap: A “Control” effect granting a contest in place of damage, with a chosen outcome (Disarm / Prone / Move).
Affects: Bearded axe, Bill / guisarme, Chain, weighted, Ranseur / spetum, Whip

▲ versatile 1-/2-handed; built as 1-handed at the listed damage

What would close the gap: A versatile flag with an alternate damage profile for the two-handed grip.
Affects: Heavy mace, Longsword / bastard sword / katana, Spear, Warhammer / horseman's pick
